## How it is read
- **The lesson of three rounds**: a creature George has seen is his. A rework keeps its vibe and changes little
  ("slight improvements to coloration and design"); Hush in 0.9.6 is the model, Glim in 0.9.6 the opposite. Every
  session this round starts from the drawing he likes and changes the least that answers his words.
- **Glim**: 0.9.5's drawing back, every frame, then Hush's kind of touches only (a second tone here and there).
  His light kept out of rain and snow (round 22's note) on that body.
- **Hush**: not touched this round, at all.
- **Quill**: 0.9.6's tabby stays; the face goes back toward 0.9.5's (small dark eyes set wide, no dark M that reads
  as a frown), which is what made him a "derpy cat-mouse".
- **Gargle**: the night sprite is the character. Awake he is that sprite with his eye open: side-on, that size,
  that silhouette. Button was not mentioned and his standing was not answered: he stays as 0.9.6 has him.
- **Tuck asleep**: the stalks themselves droop far down, the shut eyes hanging at their ends; no smile.
- **Phase**: a floating sheet: one wavy body, no head-and-body, nothing that reads as arms or hands, no horn;
  a simpler face.
- **Weather**: each creature does something about rain and snow that reads at a glance at real size.
- 0.9.6 is released and frozen. This round is **0.9.7**.
