title: HITList
---

HITList is a task manager for iPhone, iPad and Mac by George Trammell. You type tasks the way you would
say them ("Call mom tomorrow 5pm p1"), filter them with a small query language, and they sync through
your own iCloud account. It is in beta on TestFlight.
